Vision

At Ibn Khaldoun International School, our vision is to nurture a generation of world champions who, with hard work and dedication, can excel in their desired fields such as academics, sports, arts, and leadership.

In addition, our goal is to help shape students who will be equipped academically and psychologically to make significant impacts on the global stage. We also focus on catering to the interests and needs of our dear students in a healthy academic environment.

Mission

Academic Excellence

Deliver a rigorous and innovative curriculum that fosters critical thinking and problem-solving skills through modern learning strategies, technology, and academic competitions

Holistic Development

Promote physical, emotional, and intellectual growth through a balanced program of academics, arts, sports, and extracurricular activities

Character Building

Instill values of integrity, resilience, discipline, and respect to develop responsible and ethical global citizens through continuous interactions inside and outside the classroom<

Global Competence

Prepare students for a multicultural world by promoting international mindedness, cultural awareness, and proficiency in multiple languages by enriching their critical thinking skills

Leadership and Service

Encourage students to take on leadership roles and engage in community services such as assigning responsibilities within the classroom, learning from mentors and inspirational stories, and collaborating on school projects
