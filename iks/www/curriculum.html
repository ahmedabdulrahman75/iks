Acadimic Curriculum

Kindergarten 1 (KG1) – Academic Curriculum Overview
First Steps into Learning

At Ibn Khaldoun International School, our KG1 program provides a caring, creative, and engaging environment where children begin their educational journey with joy and curiosity. The curriculum focuses on early literacy, numeracy, and social-emotional development, helping young learners grow in confidence and independence.

Curriculum Framework: Skill Sharpeners Series

Core Subjects:

  • English Language: Recognizing letters and sounds, developing vocabulary, and expressing ideas through storytelling and conversation.
  • Mathematics: Learning numbers, identifying shapes, colors, patterns, and beginning comparisons (big/small, long/short).
  • Science: Observing the world through simple experiments related to plants, animals, weather, and the five senses
  • Spanish Language: Learning basic greetings, colors, and numbers through songs and games.
  • Art & Physical Education: Enhancing fine motor skills, imagination, rhythm, and movement through fun, expressive activities.

Learning Approach:
Our KG1 classrooms emphasize play-based, hands-on learning—encouraging curiosity, cooperation, and confidence as children take their first steps into education.

Kindergarten 2 (KG2) – Academic Curriculum Overview
Building Confidence and Readiness

The KG2 program at IKS prepares students for elementary school by strengthening their academic foundations and social skills. Students explore English language, math, and science in a joyful, inquiry-based environment.

Curriculum Framework: Skill Sharpeners Series

Core Subjects:

  • English Language: Building reading readiness through phonics, sight words, and sentence formation. Students develop their recognition of letter and sounds.
  • Mathematics: Counting up to 20 and beyond, simple addition and subtraction, patterns, and introduction to time and money concepts.
  • Science: Discovering living and non-living things, weather changes, and materials through hands-on exploration.
  • Spanish Language: Expanding vocabulary with topics such as animals, family, and daily routines.
  • Art & Physical Education: Nurturing self-expression, balance, coordination, and teamwork.

Learning Approach:
KG2 learners develop the confidence, curiosity, and communication skills needed to transition smoothly into KG3.

Kindergarten 3 (KG3) – Academic Curriculum Overview
Bridging Early Learning and Elementary Readiness

KG3 represents an essential bridge between early childhood and the elementary program. Students engage in structured yet dynamic lessons that develop academic readiness, independence, and social confidence

Using McGraw Hill Kindergarten-level books, our curriculum introduces children to the American academic framework in a developmentally appropriate and engaging way.

Curriculum Framework: McGraw Hill Kindergarten (Wonders, Reveal Math, Inspire Science)

Core Subjects:

  • English Language Arts (McGraw Hill Wonders): Developing early reading fluency, writing short sentences, understanding story sequence, and expanding vocabulary.
  • Mathematics (McGraw Hill Reveal Math): Numbers up to 100, patterns, addition and subtraction, measurement, and simple problem-solving.
  • Science (McGraw Hill Inspire Science): Exploring living things, the five senses, weather, and materials through hands-on experiments
  • Spanish Language: Basic conversational phrases and vocabulary for daily communication.
  • Art & Physical Education: Encouraging self-expression, creativity, rhythm, and coordination.

Learning Approach:
Through guided inquiry and playful structure, KG3 students build the skills, mindset, and enthusiasm necessary for success in Grade 1.

Grade 1 – Academic Curriculum Overview
Laying Strong Academic Foundations

Grade 1 marks a major step in a child’s academic growth. Students at IKS begin to master literacy, numeracy, and inquiry skills while developing confidence and independence.

Curriculum Framework:

  • English Language Arts: McGraw Hill Wonders
  • Mathematics: McGraw Hill Reveal Math
  • Science: McGraw Hill Inspire Science
  • Social Studies: Now and Then.

Core Subjects:

  • English Language Arts: Reading short stories, identifying main ideas, writing complete sentences, and building grammar, phonemic awareness, and vocabulary.
  • Mathematics: Understanding addition and subtraction, place value, patterns, shapes, and time.
  • Science: Observing plants, animals, weather, and matter through experiments.
  • Social Studies (Now and Then): Understanding family, community helpers, and how life changes over time.
  • Spanish Language: Basic phrases, greetings, numbers, and classroom vocabulary.
  • STEAM & Robotics:Simple problem-solving and building activities to spark creativity.
  • Art, Music & PE:Developing imagination, rhythm, and teamwork.

Grade 5 – Academic Curriculum Overview
Preparing for the Next Stage of Learning

Grade 5 at IKS is a year of independence, leadership, and readiness for middle school. Students develop advanced academic skills and global awareness.

Curriculum Framework:

  • English Language Arts: McGraw Hill Wonders
  • Mathematics: McGraw Hill Reveal Math
  • Science: McGraw Hill Inspire Science
  • Social Studies: Now and Then.

Core Subjects:

  • English Language Arts: Analytical reading, essay writing, and public speaking to build confident communication.
  • Mathematics: Mastery of fractions, decimals, volume, and real-world problem-solving.
  • Science: Studying the solar system, ecosystems, and matter through experiments and inquiry.
  • Social Studies (Now and Then): Understanding history, geography, and global interconnections.
  • Spanish Language:Advanced vocabulary, grammar, and conversation.
  • STEAM & Robotics:Applying engineering principles, coding, and creativity to innovative projects.
  • Art, Music & PE:Fostering self-expression, teamwork, and discipline.

Learning Outcome:
By the end of Grade 5, IKS students are confident, independent, and globally minded learners—ready to embrace the challenges of middle school.
